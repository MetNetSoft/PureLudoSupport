Frequently Asked Questions

How do I start a game?

Open PureLudo and choose your preferred game mode. Follow the on-screen instructions to begin.

How can I report a problem?

Send us an email and include a short description of the issue. If possible, also include your device model and iOS version.

Is an account required?

Creating an account for using PureLudo is not required. PureLudo uses Apple's native CloudKit framework.
